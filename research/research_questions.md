# Research Questions

This project is organized around practical questions that a student researcher can test honestly with public market data.

## Core Question

Can systematic equity strategies improve risk-adjusted returns compared with passive exposure to SPY?

## Strategy Questions

- Does cross-sectional momentum persist in a concentrated large-cap technology universe?
- Does a Bollinger Band mean-reversion rule identify short-term overreaction?
- Does a composite factor score based on value, momentum, quality, and growth create a more stable ranking process?

## Risk Questions

- How sensitive are strategy returns to SPY?
- Do returns remain attractive after transaction costs and slippage?
- Are drawdowns concentrated in specific market regimes?

## Modeling Questions

- Can technical and fundamental features predict future 21-trading-day returns?
- Do machine learning models improve out-of-sample prediction compared with linear regression?
- Are strategy returns explained by market, size, or value factor exposures?
